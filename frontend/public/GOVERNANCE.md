# Meridian Pulse Governance

Meridian Pulse aggregates published healthcare reporting and public SEC filings.
Headlines, excerpts, dates, and source links remain attributed to their original
publishers. Relevance tags, reasoning, urgency calculations, and filing summaries
are AI-assisted and should be checked against the linked source.

No user accounts, analytics, uploaded-file persistence, or PHI are used.
Transcript uploads are processed in memory and sent to the configured LLM provider
for analysis; uploaded source files are not retained.

For corrections, source removal, or takedown requests, contact
[amrawat@uci.edu](mailto:amrawat@uci.edu).
